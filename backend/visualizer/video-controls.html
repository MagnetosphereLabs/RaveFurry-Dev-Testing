Video and audio

In OBS, select the browser source → Interact. Drag the video to move it; drag an edge or corner to resize. It snaps near the horizontal and vertical center and keeps the incoming video’s aspect ratio.

For stream audio in the OBS mixer, enable “Control audio via OBS” on the browser source.
